# Cellvoyant

AI market and competitor intelligence for telecom: an assistant you can talk to in six languages, live public data, terminals with 450+ tools and 340+ services, 216 AI agents in 18 departments, research, a live operations console, departments and teams, a right-time customer engagement model, a market intelligence hub, workshops, resources, competitor & capital landscape, innovation lab and provisional patent draft.

## Open the live site

- **Cellvoyant**: https://nsrxg9-max.github.io/cellvoyant/
- **Cellvoyant for etisalat (e&) UAE**: https://nsrxg9-max.github.io/cellvoyant/eand.html

Both run in any browser, on desktop and phone, with nothing to install.

## What is here

Two versions share one codebase:

- `index.html`: the main Cellvoyant platform
- `eand.html`: the etisalat (e&) UAE edition, with AI agent departments, an innovation lab of 600+ tools and services, the solutions to both challenges and everything from the main platform. Not affiliated with or endorsed by etisalat (e&).

Console data is simulated for fictional operators. Research and market figures cite their sources on the page.
