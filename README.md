# Cellvoyant

AI market and competitor intelligence for telecom: research, a live operations console, departments and teams, a right-time customer engagement model, a market intelligence hub, workshops, resources, competitor & capital landscape, innovation lab and provisional patent draft.

Two versions share one codebase:

- `index.html`: the main Cellvoyant platform
- `eand.html`: the e& UAE edition, with AI agent departments, an innovation lab of 600+ tools and services, and the solutions to both challenges. Not affiliated with or endorsed by e&.

Console data is simulated for fictional operators. Research and market figures cite their sources on the page.
